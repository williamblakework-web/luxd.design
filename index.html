Approach

How the work gets done

Ownership before interface

Most hard UI problems are ownership problems wearing a UI costume. Who is accountable for this record, who is allowed to change it, and what happens when it fails. Answer that and the screen usually designs itself.

One version of the truth

Customer facing products and the back office systems behind them tend to disagree. I design for a single shared account of state across both, because two versions of the truth is where the support cost lives.

Compliance as material

Regulatory limits, AML tiers, and safer gambling constraints are not checkboxes applied after the fact. Surfaced early they are planning information. Surfaced late they are failure states.

Build it, do not describe it

Working prototypes beat annotated wireframes in every stakeholder room I have been in. I build the real thing early, in code where it helps, so decisions are made against something people can use.

Handoff is the deliverable

A Figma link is not a specification. Token tables, component inventories, and written build specs let engineering rebuild the work in any stack without making design decisions on the way.

AI used openly

Gemini for market and desk research, Claude for spec writing and rapid artifact generation. Every case study states plainly which decisions are mine and which output was machine assisted.
