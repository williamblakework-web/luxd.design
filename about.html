About

I work on the systems that carry consequences.

Eight years of client facing product design, mostly in places where a wrong interface has a cost beyond a bad review: money moving between accounts, regulatory limits, analyst decisions on a merger, a filter that half a company's reporting quietly depends on.

My work sits in complex, regulated, data heavy environments. FinTech and crypto infrastructure, AI and machine learning platforms, enterprise and internal tooling, automotive, and service design across the seam where a customer facing product meets the back office system behind it.

I run London UX Design, and I take the kind of brief that arrives as a screen request and turns out to be a question about who owns a decision.

02

How I work

Fast, and in the open. I would rather put a working prototype in front of six stakeholders on day three than circulate a deck describing one. Most of my strongest outcomes came from building the thing early enough that the argument moved from opinion to evidence.

I write specifications properly. Token tables, component inventories, layout rules, and the reasoning behind the decisions, so that engineering can build without guessing and the team that inherits the work can see why it is the way it is.

I use AI tools where they earn their place and I say so on every case study. Gemini for market and desk research. Claude for self interview spec writing and rapid artifact generation. The design decisions are mine, the machine assisted parts are labelled, and clients get to know which is which.
